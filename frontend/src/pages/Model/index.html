Training Dataset: CSE-CIC-IDS2018 (Friday Infiltration & PortScan)
Evaluation Benchmark: Offline Replay Split (4,820 contiguous slices)
Windowing Strategy: Non-overlapping Δt = 30s temporal discretization
Preprocessing: Z-score normalisation with rolling robust median imputation
Loss Objective: Next-state MSE + Contrastive Trajectory Regularisation
Random Seed: 42 (Deterministic cross-validation splits)

• Network-only visibility: Operates strictly on L3/L4 telemetry; no host-level agent introspection (EDR, process trees, registry).

• Payload opacity under TLS/SMB3 encryption: Relies on cadence, burst sizing, and sequence dynamics rather than decrypted packet contents.

• Compounding uncertainty beyond K > 4: Rollout horizons past +120s exhibit epistemic divergence and are constrained by default.
